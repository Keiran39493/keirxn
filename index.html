I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the Northbridge Institute of Technology, where I lead the Secure Learning Systems Lab. My research sits at the intersection of cybersecurity and machine learning: I study how ML-based defenses behave in the real world, how attackers evade them, and how to build detection systems that practitioners can actually deploy.

Before joining Northbridge, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Lakeshore University Security Group. I received my PhD in Computer Engineering from Westfield Polytechnic in 2021, with a thesis on adversarial robustness of network intrusion detection systems.

Outside of work, in no particular order, I am:

  • Nerd — I enjoy reading papers on the train and arguing about threat models.
  • Geek — I build mechanical keyboards and self-host far too many services.
  • Active — trail running, bouldering, and the occasional (slow) half-marathon.
  • Social — I love board-game nights and finding the best coffee in every conference city.
